# MYTHOS ARCHIVE & MYTHOS BESTIARY — Comparative World Mythology & Monsters Atlas

An offline-first, dual-realm comparative mythology & monsterology encyclopedia, learning studio, and D&D branching campaign built with **React 19 + TypeScript + Vite** in a sculpted **Obsidian, Basalt & Molten Bronze** chiaroscuro aesthetic, featuring **538 dedicated subject-matched WebP visuals** (270 Pantheon plates + 268 custom Bestiary plates) normalized to strict `4:5` (`800×1000`) portraits and `16:10` (`960×600`) widescreen scene plates plus **8 compressed HD `.mp4` cinema reels** (4 Pantheon reels + 4 Monster reels).

## Dual-Realm Architecture (`Gods & Pantheons` ↔ `Monsters & Bestiary`)
Switch instantly in the header (`?realm=pantheons` or `?realm=bestiary`) between two complete, parallel 10-page experiences across all **10 world religions**—both fully bilingual (`EN | 中文`) and compatible with the **Single-Pantheon Focus Toggle** (`?pantheon=`):
- **Realm I — Gods & Pantheons (`众神`)**: **80 deities** (**8 major deities per religion** across Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Hindu, Chinese, Japanese, Mesopotamian, Celtic, Mesoamerican, and Yoruba), **10 comparison presets**, **6 mythic motifs (35 retellings)**, **20 sacred books**, **10 D&D Apotheosis quests (30 endings)**, **10 divine family trees**, **4 ancestral mother-religions + 10×10 similarity matrix**, world diffusion map, and study studio.
- **Realm II — Monsters & Bestiary (`异兽`)**: **120 iconic dragons, chimeras, underworld hounds, shapeshifters, sky-steeds & eclipse beasts** (**12 iconic creatures per religion**) across **6 Creature Classes**, **16 monster comparison presets**, **6 parallel creature motifs (30 retellings)**, **10 bestiary codices** (*Shan Hai Jing*, *Theogony*, *Enuma Elish*, *Book of Overthrowing Apophis*, *Gazu Hyakki Yagyō*, *Florentine Codex Book XI*, *Odù Ọ̀sá Méjì*, etc.), **10 D&D Monster Hunt & Pact campaigns (30 endings)**, **10 monster brood lineage trees**, **monster transmission timeline + 10×10 matrix**, habitat map, and Monsterology study studio.

## Key Features
- **God ↔ Monster Cross-Realm Links & Unified `⌘K` Search**: 134 bidirectional links connecting iconic gods and their mythic rivals or companions (*Zeus ↔ Typhon*, *Thor ↔ Jörmungandr*, *Indra ↔ Vritra*, *Marduk ↔ Tiamat*, etc.), plus instant cross-realm search across all 200 profiles.
- **Phonetic Pronunciation Audio & Saved Codex**: 200/200 phonetic pronunciation guides with Web Speech API audio playback (`Listen`) and persistent realm-scoped bookmarks (`★ Save`).
- **Interactive D&D Campaigns & Endings Codex**: Clickable SVG overworld map nodes to rewind to any crossroads, plus an Endings Gallery tracking all 30 illustrated endings per realm with branch hints.
- **Active Learning Studio & Endless Visual Challenge**: Curated comparative seminars, visual flashcards, structured quizzes, and an unlimited dynamic portrait & lore challenge.
- **Engraved World Atlas & Print-Ready Exports**: Smooth archival coastlines, graticule lines, Compass Rose, regional figure quick-drawers, Dark/Light themes, and `@media print` PDF study sheets.
